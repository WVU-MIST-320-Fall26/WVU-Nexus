## 1. Purpose and Business Case

WVU students use more than fifteen separate apps to manage school and campus life, including eCampus, STAR, DegreeWorks, the PRT tracker, Navigate, and WVU Alert. None of them share data, so deadlines get missed and information never reaches the students who need it.

WVU Nexus puts the most important of that information in one place. Most of the data is already public or belongs to the student, so the problem can be solved in one semester without institutional system access.

## 2. Objectives and Success Criteria

| Objective | Success looks like |
|---|---|
| Ship a working product | Every feature runs on live data, not hardcoded values |
| Validate with real users | 20 or more WVU students using it before the final presentation |
| Meet accessibility standards | WCAG 2.1 AA conformance, documented |
| Protect student data | Written privacy and security design |
| Deliver on schedule | All six team deliverables submitted on time |

---

## 3. Scope

**In scope**
- Live PRT status
- Assignment tracking from the student's Blackboard calendar export
- Deadlines and grade weights pulled from uploaded syllabi
- Campus information: academic calendar, dining and library hours, alerts

**Out of scope**
- Official grade data from STAR, Banner, or DegreeWorks (requires institutional access)
- Native mobile apps (the product is a responsive web app)
- Payments, dining balances, and authentication systems

**Key milestones**

| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
Deliverable 1: repo, board, charter, RACI 
Product live with pilot users 
Team presentation 
Final product delivery
