## Strategic next steps

1. **Re-baseline Sprint 3 scope** from 71 points to approximately 25, and move the remainder to a documented post-project backlog. Decision needed before Sprint 3 planning closes.
2. **Prioritize demonstrable outcomes.** Final delivery and the team presentation are graded on what works and what real users did with it, not on backlog volume.
3. **Start pilot recruitment immediately.** It is the longest-lead item remaining and nothing else substitutes for it.
4. **Respond to the Vice Provost** with the deferral decision, the 36-point estimate, and a post-semester roadmap. The request represents the strongest institutional interest the project has attracted and the relationship should be maintained independent of the scope decision.

---

## Bottom line

A fixed deadline, a fixed team, and a scope baseline larger than both. Scope is the only available lever and we are recommending we pull it deliberately now rather than discover the shortfall at delivery. The product will ship on December 4. It will do fewer things than originally planned, and each one will work.
